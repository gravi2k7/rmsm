# 1. Introduction

Welcome to the RMSM Enterprise Trading Platform.

RMSM is an enterprise trading platform that enables traders and organizations to monitor markets, analyze opportunities, manage portfolios, and make informed trading decisions from a single workspace.

Core capabilities include:

- Real-time market monitoring
- Strategy management
- Opportunity tracking
- Decision support
- Order management
- Portfolio analytics
- Notifications
- Secure authentication

---

# 2. Getting Started

## System Requirements

Supported browsers:

- Google Chrome
- Microsoft Edge
- Mozilla Firefox
- Safari (latest versions)

Recommended:

- Large monitor (1920×1080 or higher)
- Stable internet connection

---

## Accessing RMSM

Open your browser and navigate to your organization's RMSM URL.

Example:

```text
https://trader.company.com
```

---

# 3. Logging In

Enter:

- Email Address
- Password

Optional:

- Remember Me

Select:

**Login**

If you forget your password:

1. Select **Forgot Password**.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Follow the reset instructions.

---

## Two-Factor Authentication

If enabled:

1. Enter your username and password.
2. Enter the verification code from your authenticator application.
3. Access the platform.

# 5. Market Watch

The Market Watch screen displays available trading instruments.

Features:

- Search instruments
- View latest prices
- View market status
- Open TradingView charts
- Add instruments to watchlists

Typical workflow:

1. Search for an instrument.
2. Open the chart.
3. Add to a watchlist.
4. Continue monitoring.

The workspace is designed to minimize navigation during active trading.

# 16. Frequently Asked Questions

## I forgot my password.

Use the **Forgot Password** option on the login page.

---

## Why can't I access a feature?

Your role may not have permission to access that functionality.

Contact your administrator.

---

## Why can't I see market data?

Possible causes:

- Market closed
- Network issue
- Data provider unavailable
- Insufficient permissions

---

Navigate to:

Profile → Settings

# 17. Troubleshooting

## Login Problems

Verify:

- Email
- Password
- Internet connection

If the problem persists, contact your administrator.

---

## Charts Not Loading

Check:

- Internet connection
- Browser compatibility
- Refresh the page

---

## Slow Performance

Try:

- Refreshing the browser
- Closing unused tabs
- Clearing browser cache

---

## Missing Data

Possible causes:

- Permission restrictions
- Temporary service issue
- Data synchronization delay

---

# 18. Support

If you experience problems:

1. Review this guide.
2. Contact your organization administrator.
3. Provide:

- Username
- Date and time
- Error message
- Steps to reproduce

Screenshots can help diagnose UI-related issues.

# Best Practices

To get the most from RMSM:

- Review your dashboard at the start of each trading session.
- Maintain organized watchlists.
- Monitor notifications regularly.
- Keep your profile information up to date.
- Enable two-factor authentication if available.
- Review portfolio performance periodically.
- Log out when using shared devices.
